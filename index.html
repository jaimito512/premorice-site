About

Premo Rice, also known as Mr. Rice and The Bishop, blends funk, soul and hip hop. He started as a producer in 2013, selling beats through Vine, then began rapping on his own production after hearing other artists underdeliver on it.

He joined Larry June's tour in 2019 and played Day N Vegas in 2021. The Rice Tape reached the Apple Music Top 50 with no major label push.
